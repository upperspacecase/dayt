# Dayt Knight — Brand & Rules

The running rule list for Dayt Knight. **Add a line the moment a rule is set. Read this before writing any copy, offer, or product decision.** Newest rules can go at the top of their section.

## Voice & copy
- **Never use the word "cheap" / "cheapest".** We're premium. Lead with the experience, not the price tag.
- No emojis. Anywhere.
- Banned filler words: every, actually, truly, simply, just.
- No fabricated numbers or social proof — real metrics only, or none. (No invented "47 locals".)
- Be specific, not vague: name the spot, the hour, the detail. "Roosevelt Island tram at 7:40" beats "a scenic ride".

## Positioning
- **The belief (north star):** dating should be fun and magic for everyone — and the admin (planning, logistics, figuring out what to do) shouldn't get in the way of connecting. The enemy is admin friction.
- We are not "date ideas" and not entertainment. We are: the place where dating feels magic again, with the admin removed.
- Don't anchor low. Never compare to Netflix or frame as a budget app.
- The mental account is what they already spend on date nights + couples therapy + the cost of drifting apart — not low-cost alternatives.

## Product model
- **Two layers, one belief.** The engine = the magic (generates *complete* date concepts — the whole thing, how it comes together — for inspiration). The people = the done-for-you (creators add concepts + profiles, sell as one-off ideas or full concierge).
- **Free:** 3 fresh engine ideas, new each day, on the landing page. A reason to come back daily.
- **The Club:** unlimited ideas + filter (area / vibe / budget) + build-your-own to sell.
- The connection science is the **kitchen, not the menu.** Don't show users a formula; don't over-prescribe. Each idea just feels like magic.
- The growth loop is the product's own action: "send this to someone you'd do it with."

## Offer & monetization (Hormozi lens)
- **The Club** is the recurring offer (unlimited + filter + a place to earn). Don't anchor it low or frame it as a budget app.
- **Concierge / one-offs** are premium, delivered by real creators in the marketplace; their price is set by who's delivering it. Platform takes a cut (Stripe Connect ~70/30).
- Charge for the transformation, not the tool. Every guarantee names a specific failure + a specific remedy. Real scarcity only (where a human is the constraint); never fake urgency.

- Keep it simple. Don't be over-prescriptive.
