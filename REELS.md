# Reels — batch 1

Tay shoots and posts. Follows `BRAND.md`: specific, premium, no emojis, no "cheap", every reel ends on a version of *send this to someone you'd do it with* (the share is the product's core action). Hooks are the first 2 seconds — they decide everything. Keep cuts fast.

---

## 1. Golden Hour Tram (the oddly-specific one)
- **Hook (0–2s):** "The best skyline view in New York, and almost no one takes a date here"
- **Shots:** POV stepping into the red tram car → doors close → it lifts over the East River → sun on the skyline → two coffees on the rail
- **Text beats:** "Roosevelt Island tram." / "Go at 7:40, not before." / "Bring two coffees." / "Say nothing for the first 30 seconds. Just watch."
- **Caption:** "Roosevelt Island tram, golden hour. Send this to someone you'd ride it with."

## 2. The One Question (most shareable — shows the magic)
- **Hook:** "One question that turns a normal date into a real one"
- **Shots:** a couple mid-date (walking, a table, a view) → cut to the question on screen → their reaction
- **The question on screen:** "What's something you've wanted to try and never told anyone?"
- **Text beat:** "Ask it halfway through, not at the start."
- **Caption:** "Save this for your next date. Send it to the person you'd ask."

## 3. POV: you didn't have to plan it (for the exhausted planner)
- **Hook:** "POV: for once, you weren't the one who planned the date"
- **Shots:** phone buzzes with a text — where, when, booked → cut to them just showing up → a great night unfolds
- **Text beat:** "Imagine if every Friday looked like this and you did nothing."
- **Caption:** "If you're always the one who plans it… send this to them."

## 4. The Midnight Ferry (free + cinematic)
- **Hook:** "A free boat past the Statue of Liberty that nobody thinks to take on a date"
- **Shots:** boarding the Staten Island Ferry at night → the skyline pulling away from the back rail → the Statue lit up → wind in hair
- **Text beats:** "Whitehall Terminal, after dark." / "Back rail for the skyline. Front for the Statue." / "Ride there, slice on Staten Island, ride straight back."
- **Caption:** "The midnight ferry. Send this to someone you'd stand at the rail with."

## 5. Bad at it together (Ceramics)
- **Hook:** "The secret to a good date is being bad at something together"
- **Shots:** two people at a pottery wheel, hands covered in clay, a bowl collapsing, both laughing
- **Text beats:** "Beginner wheel-throwing." / "The bowl will be ugly. That's the point." / "Toast the worst one at the bar next door."
- **Caption:** "Send this to the person you'd make a mess with."

## 6. Beat the city awake (Sunrise bridge)
- **Hook:** "Set an absurd alarm and do this once"
- **Shots:** empty Brooklyn Bridge before dawn → sky going pink over Manhattan → two coffees → breakfast after
- **Text beat:** "The bridge is empty at 5:30am. It's yours."
- **Caption:** "Sunrise on the bridge. Send this to someone you'd lose sleep for."

## 7. Off-season Coney (oddball/nostalgic)
- **Hook:** "Coney Island in the cold is the most romantic place in New York and nobody believes me"
- **Shots:** empty boardwalk, grey sky → a shared hot dog → one ride running, the two of them on it
- **Text beat:** "Empty. Cold. Yours."
- **Caption:** "Send this to someone you'd freeze on a boardwalk with."

## 8. Stop being roommates (the belief / manifesto)
- **Hook:** "When did 'what do you want to do' become the most dangerous sentence in your relationship?"
- **Shots:** couple on a couch, phones out, Netflix glow → hard cut to them out in the city, lit up, laughing
- **Text beats:** "The planning is the worst part." / "So we removed it." / "Three new ways to fall for each other, new each day."
- **Caption:** "Dating should be fun and magic. The admin shouldn't get in the way. daytknight.club — send this to the one you'd do it all with."
